A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was a famous Indian scientist and the 11th President of India. He was popularly known as the "Missile Man of India."

Early Life

  • Born on 15 October 1931.
  • He was born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.
  • He came from a humble background.
  • He worked hard to achieve his dreams.

Scientific Career

  • He worked as an aerospace scientist.
  • He worked with ISRO and DRDO.
  • He played an important role in India's space and missile programs.

President of India

  • He became the 11th President of India.
  • He served as President from 2002 to 2007.
  • He was loved and respected by students across India.

Conclusion

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam continues to inspire students and young people through his life, achievements and dedication to India.
