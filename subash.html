Subash Chandra Bose

Subash Chandra Bose was one of the most prominent leaders of India's freedom movement. He was popularly known as Netaji.

Early Life

  • Born on 23 January 1897.
  • He was born in Cuttack, Odisha.
  • He was a brilliant student and leader.

Role in India's Freedom Struggle

  • He strongly opposed British rule in India.
  • He led the Indian National Army (INA).
  • He inspired thousands of Indians to fight for independence.
  • He is remembered for his courage and patriotism.

Famous Slogan

"Give me blood, and I will give you freedom."

Conclusion

Subash Chandra Bose remains an inspiration for generations because of his courage, leadership and dedication to India's independence.
